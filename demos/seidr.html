Seiðr

In Norse cosmology, seiðr was a form of magic practiced by the völva — the wandering shaman-seeress. Through ritual trance induced by sacred plants, drumming, and chant, she could travel between the nine worlds of Yggdrasil and return with visions of fate.


The mushroom — perhaps Amanita muscaria, the fly agaric — was known to shamanic cultures across the circumpolar north. Its consumption broke the veil between worlds.

Völva

The völva carried a staff (völr) and wore ritual garments adorned with amber, bone runes, and the pelts of animal spirits. She was both feared and revered — capable of seeing the threads of wyrd, the web of fate woven by the Norns beneath Yggdrasil.


When the mushroom opened her inner eye, the runes did not need to be read — they appeared burning behind the retina, carved into the fabric of sight itself.
